Year 2026

Live Site View Live →

Overview

This project was created to build a web-based boutique storefront for an eco-conscious fashion brand as part of an assignment. Everloop Boutique is an eco-conscious fashion storefront built and deployed on Netlify. The site promotes sustainable shopping by offering hand-curated pre-loved and upcycled clothing, accessories, and jewelry — positioning secondhand fashion as a stylish, environmentally responsible alternative to buying new.

The site features a clean, nature-inspired design with a minimalist aesthetic, showcasing featured pieces with pricing, an impact metrics section highlighting environmental benefits like garments saved and water conserved, and a "Why Choose Everloop" section outlining the boutique's core values. A community-facing mission ties it together — 10% of profits support local environmental initiatives.

Building Everloop reinforced how thoughtful layout and copywriting can communicate brand identity as effectively as visual design. The project also emphasized structuring a product-focused site to guide visitors from discovery to intent — balancing aesthetics with clear, purposeful content.
