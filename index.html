About Me

Hello! My name is Daphney, and I am a passionate designer and creative that loves bringing ideas to life through design. I'm currently majoring in Digital Media (Web and Interactive Media Track) at the University of Central Florida.

I enjoy transforming abstract ideas into tangible designs, using imagination as a starting point and design as the tool that brings those ideas to life. I’m often inspired by drawing and visual art, books on fiction and by the emotional power of film and music in storytelling in my personal work. I have a soft spot for animals, things that antique and vintage, and objects with history and character

I am looking for opportunities where I can grow as a designer and contribute and collaborate with others as a team.
